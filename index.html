A corrupted AI scrambles your radar. Survive escalating waves with limited visibility and hack terminals to restore your HUD.

  • • Fog of war on every map
  • • Bonus XP for stealth rescues

Weapons & Guns

Build a loadout from 30+ guns: assault rifles, precision railguns, flame shotguns, and experimental energy weapons. Mods let you toggle between crowd control or boss shredding.

Earn heals by protecting civilians, then deploy med-stations, revive pulses, and shield domes. Support characters can stack buffs and keep the squad alive during boss waves.

Save the People

Rescue survivors from collapse zones, escort them through breaches, and evacuate them with dropships. Every life saved grants rare upgrades and unlocks new districts.
