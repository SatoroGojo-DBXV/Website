Good questions.
Simple answers.

Choosing a tutor is a personal decision. Here’s where to start.

How do I choose the right tutor?

Start with your child’s class, board, subject and goals. Discuss teaching style, qualifications, availability and fees before agreeing to lessons. A trial lesson, if offered, can help you assess the fit.

Can I choose between home and online classes?

Yes. Select your preferred mode in the enquiry builder. Home-tuition availability depends on your locality; online lessons offer more flexibility around location.

What will tuition cost?

Fees are not published yet. They depend on class level, subject, lesson frequency, mode and tutor experience. Confirm the full fee and cancellation terms before committing.

Which school boards can I enquire about?

You can specify CBSE, ICSE / ISC, a State Board, IB or Cambridge in your request. A suitable tutor’s curriculum experience and availability need to be confirmed.

Does this website send my enquiry?

This first version prepares a downloadable enquiry only. It does not send your details to DTB or book a lesson. Direct submissions will be available once DTB’s contact channel is connected.
